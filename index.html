HOW TO PLAY

WASD / Arrows — move · SPACE / SHIFT — dash (brief invincibility) · E — ability · P / ESC — pause

Your gun fires at the nearest hostile automatically. You position, you dash, you survive.

Every boss attack is telegraphed — gold flash, dashed lines, pool outlines. The dashed red line of a charge shows the EXACT path and its stop marker shows where the boss will land. Nothing hurts you without warning. Dash THROUGH beams, not away from them.

Phases make bosses angrier: every phase they fire sooner and faster, and the transition itself answers with a gold ring — slower than your run, so keep moving.

Every boss has a signature: a named gimmick only it owns — homing spore drift, mines that burst when crowded, halos that circle and burst, walls with one gap, shells lobbed at marked squares, a pull that drags your line, a mark on the square you stand on, a teleport step, ricochet slugs, pools that bloom. The announcer names it before every round; sharing attacks is fine, sharing an identity is not.

Mutators: every queue can roll house twists — FRAIL, SWIFT HORROR, SIEGE, HEATED AIR, BROOD BLIGHT, VOLATILE CORE, ADRENALINE, BOUNTY. TEMPO is the boss's pacing: ×1.25 cycles its attacks a quarter sooner, ×0.9 pauses a little longer — higher is always faster. Bronze mostly stays clean; the deep ranks stack two. Each round opens with a countdown that names the boss and every mutator with what it does — read it, then FIGHT. No two rounds play the same.

Ranked: each queue rolls a random boss from your rank's pool. Wins bank SR by the hearts you keep; flatlining costs SR. Higher ranks send the same lieutenants with more HP and tighter pacing — Silver walls you with the DEATHWARRANT weight class. GHOST unlocks at Silver (500 SR), BULWARK at Platinum (1100 SR).

Accounts: REGISTER in the hub header to bank your progress under a callsign — passcode stored only as a salted hash, in this browser (the Pit is a static site; there is no server). Registering claims this browser's existing progress automatically, so nothing is lost. The Top 5 board is honest: every slot reads ??? until a real registered pilot on this browser cracks 4000 SR — no fake names, ever.

Game modes: logged-in pilots get a mode bar above the ladder. RANKED is the ladder exactly as always — SR on the line, bosses roll from your rank's pool. CASUAL is practice: pick any boss you've already met in ranked and refight it at that rank's pacing — no SR, no W/L, nothing changes. The casual roster is your ranked history: locked bosses read ??? until you fight them in the ladder first.
