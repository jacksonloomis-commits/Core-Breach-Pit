HOW TO PLAY

WASD / Arrows — move · SPACE / SHIFT — dash (brief invincibility) · E — ability · P — pause

Your gun fires at the nearest hostile automatically. You position, you dash, you survive.

Every boss attack is telegraphed — gold flash, dashed lines, pool outlines. Nothing hurts you without warning. Dash THROUGH beams, not away from them.

Stars: 3 = no hits taken · 2 = two hits or fewer · 1 = cleared. Clear an arena to open the next. Clear Arena II for GHOST, Arena IV for BULWARK.
